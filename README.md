### 📄 Research (3 accepted papers)
- **Representational Structure of ConceptARC Transformations in Pretrained Vision Models**, *NeurIPS 2026 Dev AI Workshop* ([code](https://github.com/anabhide/ConceptARC-Transformation-Geometry)). We represented each ARC-style puzzle as the direction between its input and output embeddings in frozen CLIP and DINOv2. These directions were more similar for puzzles sharing a concept than for different ones, and let us identify the concept family of held-out puzzles at up to 47.5% accuracy (6.25% chance), beating static embeddings and a large hand-built feature set.
- **Stacking-Based Weighting for Large Language Bayes in M-Open Settings**, *ICML 2026 Workshop on Epistemic Intelligence in ML* ([code](https://github.com/Akarsh-dundun/Large-Language-Bayes-Model)). Instead of Bayesian model averaging betting on a single model, this method spreads weight across models while matching predictive accuracy under misspecification.
- **Extending Causal Metamodeling to a Non-Markovian Queue**, *Winter Simulation Conference 2026* ([code](https://github.com/PrachetaBA/non-markovian-mdbn)). Modular dynamic Bayesian networks used as simulation metamodels that answer probabilistic and interventional queries 42-99x faster than Monte Carlo simulation, within a mean Jensen-Shannon distance of 0.03-0.05 of ground truth.
